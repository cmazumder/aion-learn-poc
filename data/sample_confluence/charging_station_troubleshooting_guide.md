# Charging Station Troubleshooting Guide

## Introduction

This guide provides instructions for troubleshooting common issues with our charging stations. Please follow these steps before escalating an issue to the engineering team.

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Slow Charging

1. **Check the power source:** Ensure that the circuit breaker is not tripped and that the station is receiving the correct voltage.
1. **Inspect the charging cable:** Look for any visible damage to the charging cable or connector. A damaged cable can lead to reduced charging speed.
1. **Check for firmware updates:** Ensure that the station is running the latest firmware version. A recent firmware update may have addressed performance issues.
1. **Review the station logs:** The station logs may contain error messages or warnings that can help diagnose the issue. Look for any messages related to thermal throttling or power management.

### Payment Issues

1. **Reboot the payment terminal:** A simple reboot can often resolve issues with unresponsive payment terminals.
1. **Check the network connection:** Ensure that the payment terminal is connected to the network and can reach the payment gateway.
1. **Inspect the card reader:** Clean the card reader and check for any foreign objects that may be obstructing it.
1. **Try a different payment method:** If possible, try using a different credit card or payment method to rule out an issue with the user's card.

### Connectivity Problems

1. **Check the cellular signal strength:** The station's diagnostic menu should display the current cellular signal strength. A weak signal can cause intermittent connectivity issues.
1. **Reboot the modem:** Power cycling the cellular modem can often resolve connectivity problems.
1. **Inspect the antenna:** Ensure that the cellular antenna is securely connected and is not damaged.
1. **Check for network outages:** Check with the cellular provider to see if there are any network outages in the area.

## Escalation Procedures

If you have followed the steps in this guide and are still unable to resolve the issue, please escalate the issue to the engineering team by creating a JIRA ticket. Please include the following information in the ticket:

- Station ID
- A detailed description of the issue
- The steps you have already taken to troubleshoot the issue
- Any relevant logs or error messages
